# RavenTech OSINT Commercial Positioning

## Product Position

RavenTech OSINT is an internal, defensive investigation and case-management
platform. It helps authorized security teams organize passive infrastructure
evidence, threat reputation, deterministic findings, remediation activity,
analyst collaboration, governance, and professional reporting.

## Primary Value

- Consolidates authorized passive recon into investigation context.
- Converts stored evidence into deterministic, auditable findings.
- Connects findings to remediation tasks and defensive playbooks.
- Preserves citations, analyst notes, ownership, and workflow history.
- Produces audience-specific PDF, DOCX, HTML, and Markdown reports.
- Provides RBAC, audit, retention metadata, export controls, and feature flags.
- Uses local defensive knowledge and clearly identifies optional AI availability.

## Intended Users

- defensive OSINT analysts
- SOC and incident-response teams
- exposure-management teams
- security consultants operating under written authorization
- internal risk, governance, and remediation stakeholders

## Responsible Positioning

Use these terms:

- defensive assessment
- authorized scope
- passive recon
- evidence-backed finding
- remediation workflow
- analyst review
- governance-controlled archive

Avoid claims that the platform proves compromise without supporting evidence.
The platform is not an exploitation framework, autonomous agent, active scanner,
or internet-wide enumeration service.

## Demonstration Narrative

1. Define authorized scope in an investigation.
2. Collect passive evidence for a permitted target.
3. Generate explainable defensive findings.
4. Assign review and remediation work.
5. Apply a defensive playbook.
6. Produce an audience-appropriate report with citations.
7. Review governance, audit, and operational readiness.

## V1 Portfolio Message

RavenTech OSINT is a portfolio-ready defensive investigation workspace. The
strongest demo story is not automation for its own sake; it is analyst control:
authorized scope, passive evidence, explainable findings, remediation ownership,
governance, and professional report delivery.

For the 5.0.0-rc1 demo, use the synthetic `[DEMO]` workspace and the
`PORTFOLIO_DEMO_FLOW.md` script. Present demo data as training/sample data only.

Position V1 as:

- an internal analyst workspace
- a defensive intelligence organizer
- a case-management and reporting layer
- a governance-aware evidence system

Avoid presenting V1 as a scanner, exploit platform, attribution engine, breach
proof system, or autonomous SOC replacement.

## Deployment Boundary

The current platform is suited to controlled internal or evaluation
deployments. Production adoption should include organization-specific security
review, backup procedures, monitoring, secret management, retention approval,
and operational ownership.
